What the portal does for you

  • Replaces the emails and spreadsheets we used to send transfers back and forth — everything lives in one place with a clear status
  • Auto-fills patient info from a PK Transfer Sheet PDF — just drop the sheet in, verify, submit
  • Tracks FedEx automatically — when you type the Transfer ID (or bulk shipment ID) into WorldShip's Customer Reference field, tracking + delivery + status flow back to the app on their own
  • Routes questions to the right pharmacy's Teams chat with a one-click "❓ Ask question" button
  • Handles bulk shipments end-to-end — pack, print packing slip with barcode, mark received, flag missing scripts
