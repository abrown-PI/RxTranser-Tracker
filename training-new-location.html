PI Transfer Tracker — Getting Started

A day-one guide for [LOCATION] as a receiving pharmacy.

Welcome to the PI Transfer Tracker! This is the portal we use across all PI pharmacies to move prescriptions between stores. For [LOCATION], you'll mostly use it to receive shipments from Erie (compounds) or other PI stores, process refills on transfers that were originally set up by another location, and hand off prepared prescriptions to patients who come in for pickup.

Day-One Setup (5 minutes)

  1. Open the site in Microsoft Edge (our standard browser).
  2. Click Sign in with Microsoft 365 — use your @pharmacyinnovations.net account (same one you use for Outlook and Teams).
  3. Your location should default to [LOCATION]. If it says something else, look at the location dropdown in the navbar to switch — or ping Ashley to fix your assignment.
  4. Bookmark the site.
  5. Click 📖 Help in the navbar and skim the 🆕 What's New section at the top so you know the current button labels.

What You'll See When You Sign In

Two tabs matter most for a receiving pharmacy:

  • Transfers — every prescription heading TO [LOCATION] (from any origin, any fill pharmacy). This is your inbound queue.
  • Shipments — the physical boxes coming to you. Green ← Incoming chip means "on its way here."

The blue banner just under the navbar tells you when your next shipment is expected — computed from [LOCATION]'s configured receiving day(s).

The Alerts tab shows questions other locations have asked you, and any transfer of yours that's stuck in one status too long.

The Four Things You'll Do Most Often

1. Receive a shipment — the normal case (everything in the box)

  1. Go to the Shipments tab
  2. Focus the 📷 Scan tracking barcode field at the top of the page
  3. Scan either the FedEx barcode on the box OR the barcode on the bulk packing slip inside
  4. The shipment opens → click 📦 Arrived at my pharmacy
  5. Check off each Rx as you unpack it (or click Select all if the whole manifest checks out)
  6. Take a photo with the camera button if you see any damage
  7. Click Mark Package Received

Every transfer in the box automatically flips to Received. Done.

2. Receive a shipment — some scripts are missing (partial receive)

This happens more often than you'd think — a script gets added to the packing slip but doesn't physically make it into the box. Do not check every box and mark it fully received. Use partial receive so the fill pharmacy knows to look.

  1. Open the shipment like normal (scan the barcode or click into it from Shipments)
  2. Click 📦 Arrived at my pharmacy
  3. Check off ONLY the Rx you actually have in front of you
  4. Leave the missing ones unchecked
  5. Click Receive Partial (this button appears once at least one item is checked and at least one is not)
  6. Confirm — the checked items flip to Received, the unchecked ones get flagged as missing on the transfer

What happens next:

  • The fill pharmacy sees the missing items highlighted on the shipment and gets a Teams notification
  • If a whole patient's shipment was missing (all their Rx unchecked), that transfer is automatically pulled off the box, reverted to In Progress, and put back on the fill queue for the next shipment — no action needed from you
  • For partial patient shipments (some Rx received, some missing), the fill pharmacy will click × Reship missing on their end to pull the missing script off and reship it in the next box
Do not "check everything" if something is missing. If you mark the whole box as received, the missing scripts get lost in the shuffle — the app thinks they arrived. Use Receive Partial every time there's a discrepancy, no matter how small.

3. Refill an existing transfer (the patient is due for a refill)

Always use +Refill for a refill. Never use +New Transfer. The original transfer already has the PK sheet, the Rx numbers, the shipping preferences, and the payment info. Using +Refill keeps the new fill chained to the original so the whole Rx history stays connected.

From the +Refill tab

  1. Click + Refill at the top (next to + New Transfer)
  2. Search by patient name, Origin Rx#, or Receiving Rx#
  3. Click Refill this → on the right transfer
  4. Review the Shipping & Payment Defaults (auto-pulled from the original). Click Edit if anything changed (new address, different billing).
  5. If the patient has multiple Rx on the original, check the boxes for which Rx to include in this refill (they may only need one refilled, not all of them)
  6. Click Continue to refill form →
  7. The form opens pre-filled — PK sheet, hard copies, formula, and Rx numbers all carry over. Review, replace any doc if something changed, then click Submit.

From the transfer detail (one-click)

Open any transfer for that patient → click 📋 Refill this Rx at the top. Same carry-over behavior, one fewer click.

What if the original transfer isn't in the app?

For legacy patients whose first fill pre-dates the portal:

  1. Click + Refill → scroll past the search results
  2. Click + Enter Refill Manually in the dashed box at the bottom
  3. Fill in whatever you have for the original (prior pharmacy, prior Rx #, notes — all optional)
  4. Then fill out the rest like a new transfer and submit

The Prior Transfer Info shows up on the detail modal so context is preserved. This option goes away naturally as your patients cycle through their first fill in the portal.

4. Non-transfer pickup — a prepared script is coming for a patient to pick up at [LOCATION]

This is when another pharmacy (usually Erie, for compounds) prepares a script and ships it to [LOCATION] so the patient can pick it up in person here. It's NOT a real transfer — you don't put anything in your dispensing system, you just hand off the prepared scripts when the patient walks in.

You'll see these in your Transfers tab with a yellow 📍 Non-transfer badge under the From→Fill column. Erie is doing the compounding but [LOCATION] is doing the pickup, so it shows up on your worklist.

When the box arrives, receive it the same way as a normal bulk shipment (steps 1 or 2 above). When the patient walks in for pickup:

  1. Open the transfer from the Transfers tab (or search by patient name)
  2. Click Mark Picked Up and have the patient sign on-screen (works with mouse, touchscreen, or stylus)

Asking & Answering Questions

Asking another pharmacy a question

You need info from the fill pharmacy or the origin pharmacy about a specific transfer:

  1. From the Transfers list, find the patient → click ❓ Ask question
  2. Type your question → Send Question
  3. A card lands in that pharmacy's Teams chat. They reply in the transfer's Discussion section.
  4. You'll see their reply on your Alerts tab → "Questions You've Asked"

Answering a question another pharmacy asked you

  1. Open the Alerts tab
  2. Under Open Questions you'll see anything waiting on you
  3. Click Answer → to open the transfer
  4. Type your reply in the Discussion section

The number badge on the Alerts tab = open questions for you + overdue transfers combined.

The Active view (default) hides anything that's already Received or Canceled — so your list stays focused on what's actually inbound. Flip to Complete or All if you're hunting for a specific transfer you already received.

Payment Status (Tabz auto-sync + Paid in Store)

Most payment activity updates automatically when the patient pays through Tabz — you don't have to do anything. The Payment column on the Transfers table shows the rolled-up status for each transfer:

  • Pending — no payment activity yet
  • 🛒 Tabz Cart Created — Tabz has built a cart for the patient (text/email sent). Waiting on payment.
  • Partial — some Rx paid, some pending
  • ✓ Paid (Tabz) — patient paid through Tabz
  • ✓ Paid in Store — someone at a pharmacy manually marked it paid

When a patient walks in and pays at the counter (not through Tabz), click the small 💲 icon in the Actions column on that transfer and mark it Paid in Store.

Ship-To Address Reminders

Two flavors of receiving:

  • Ship To = Pharmacy — box comes to [LOCATION]. Patient picks up (or is delivered from your store). Most common for compounds and refills.
  • Ship To = Patient — box goes directly to the patient's home. [LOCATION] doesn't receive anything for these; they show up in your Transfers list for visibility but there's no receive step.
Temporary address on a Patient ship-to? If the patient set a temporary address (vacation, snowbird), you'll see a warning banner on the transfer telling you NOT to update the patient's permanent record. Just fulfill this one at the temp address and leave the permanent record alone.

Things NOT To Do

❌ Do not enter a refill as a new transfer. Always use + Refill and search for the original. Entering it as +New Transfer breaks the Rx History chain and creates a duplicate patient record for the fill pharmacy to clean up.
❌ Do not mark a bulk shipment fully received if any scripts are missing. Use Receive Partial and only check what you actually have. Otherwise the missing ones are lost — the app thinks they arrived and the fill pharmacy has no reason to look.
❌ Do not close out a non-transfer pickup order until the patient actually picks it up. Keep it in your queue so you don't forget it's waiting on the shelf. Use the Mark Picked Up button + signature capture when the patient walks in.
